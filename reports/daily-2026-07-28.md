### NCVER

**VET participation remains high, while qualification enrolments fall**
*Published: 2026-07-22 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

NCVER's Total VET Students and Courses 2025 report shows 5.1 million Australians enrolled in nationally recognised VET last year — participation at record levels — but full qualification enrolments fell more than 5%, with declines across all jurisdictions and all provider types including TAFEs and private RTOs. The biggest drops were in Certificate III and IV, particularly in government-funded training, with community services, business services, and tourism, travel and hospitality hit hardest. Shorter courses and standalone subjects grew, especially in fee-for-service training.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Full qualification enrolments in VET fell more than 5% in 2025 (NCVER data) — Cert III and IV hardest hit. RTOs pivoting toward shorter courses and fee-for-service are moving with the market, not against it.
