### NCVER

**Trade apprentices completing at higher rates**
*Published: 2026-07-28 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

New NCVER data shows trade apprentice completion rates reached 58.7% for the 2019 commencing cohort — a steady improvement since 2015. Electrician completions rose to 71.7% and carpenter/joiner to 63.7%. In contrast, non-trade apprenticeship and traineeship completion rates declined for a third consecutive cohort. Completion rates for apprentices with disability declined and remain among the lowest recorded; regional and remote apprentices continued to outperform the national average.

Content-worthy: YES
