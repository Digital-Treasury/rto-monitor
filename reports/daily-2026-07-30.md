### ASIC Newsroom

**Federal Court imposes permanent director's disqualification order against Larry Dawson**
*Published: 2026-07-30 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Federal Court of Australia permanently disqualified NSW director Larry Dawson from managing corporations after he breached his directors' duties as sole director of PW Kitt Co Pty Ltd, facilitating a scheme that defrauded investors of approximately $7 million in superannuation funds. His conduct included setting up company bank accounts and enabling cryptocurrency transfers used to dissipate investor funds without any oversight. This reinforces that RTO directors — as company directors — carry real personal liability if they passively allow their company to be misused, even without direct involvement.
