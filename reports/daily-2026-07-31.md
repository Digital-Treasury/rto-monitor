### ASQA Latest News

**Principles for the Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in VET released**
*Published: 2026-07-30 | Relevance: HIGH*

ASQA released 5 Principles for the Responsible Use of AI in VET, applying to all aspects of a provider's operations. Each principle includes a description, self-assurance questions, and case studies to guide implementation. The principles do not introduce new regulatory requirements — they are a structured framework for interpreting existing obligations under the 2025 Standards for RTOs. The document was informed by sector consultation through ASQA's 2026 Sector Workshops, so the framing reflects what ASQA auditors will likely reference when AI-related compliance questions arise.

Content-worthy: YES
