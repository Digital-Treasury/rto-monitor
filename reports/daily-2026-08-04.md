### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC disqualifies Victorian director Antonio Torcasio for 5 years**
*Published: 2026-08-04 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has disqualified Melbourne-based Antonio Torcasio from managing corporations for the maximum five-year period, citing his involvement in the failure of eight companies. ASIC pursues directors with a pattern of company insolvencies on grounds including insolvent trading, failure to maintain books/records, and failure to meet lodgement obligations. RTO owners who also hold directorships in other entities should note this as a reminder that a pattern of company failures draws ASIC scrutiny regardless of the industry involved.

Content-worthy: YES
