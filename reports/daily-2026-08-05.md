### ASQA Latest News

**ASQA and TEQSA sign MOU focused on more efficient quality for dual sector providers**
*Published: 2026-08-05 | Relevance: HIGH*

ASQA and TEQSA have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to coordinate regulatory oversight of dual-sector providers — those registered with both regulators. The agreement establishes a Joint Strategic Oversight Committee and aims to align evidence requirements, reduce duplicate reporting, and produce joint guidance. Practical changes will include reduced need for providers to submit similar information twice and better-aligned audit processes between the two agencies.

### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC launches new digital resources for small business directors**
*Published: 2026-08-05 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has launched a "Small Business Director Essentials" hub — a free online resource combining practical guidance, learning modules and tools for company directors at all stages: planning, setup, operation, restructuring and closure. The hub is directly relevant for RTO owners and operators who hold director roles under the Corporations Act and have ASIC compliance obligations alongside their ASQA obligations.

**ASIC launches small business strategy, helping to educate and protect small businesses**
*Published: 2026-08-05 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC released a refreshed Small Business Strategy setting out its approach to supporting and protecting small businesses through practical education, simpler interactions, stronger engagement and targeted enforcement. The strategy is the policy framework behind the Director Essentials hub released the same day. It signals ASIC intends to be more accessible to small business directors while also increasing enforcement scrutiny of misconduct that harms small businesses.
