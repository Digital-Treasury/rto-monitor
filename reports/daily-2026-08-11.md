### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC protects consumers by removing high-risk financial sector participants**
*Published: 2026-08-10 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC delivered 150 administrative enforcement outcomes in 2025-26, including 36 director disqualifications from managing corporations — a figure ASIC describes as rising sharply from the prior financial year. The annual summary also includes 87 removals from providing financial services and 27 from credit services. While the focus is on the financial sector, the director disqualification data is directly relevant to RTO owners, who hold company director obligations under the Corporations Act alongside their training obligations.
