### ASQA Latest News

**Data Provision Requirements amendments**
*Published: 2026-08-12 | Relevance: HIGH*

DEWR and NCVER have published the National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Data Provision Requirements) Amendment (Data Streamlining) Instrument 2026, which comes into effect on 1 October 2026. This instrument replaces the AVETMISS VET Provider Collection Specifications with a new VET Information Standard, changing how RTOs collect and submit activity data. Most existing RTOs (registered before 1 Jan 2028 with no State/Territory Training Authority arrangement) can continue reporting under AVETMISS until they choose to transition, but must have done so by 1 January 2029. The instrument also introduces new data system requirements: RTOs must store reportable data digitally and support reporting through the required pathway.

### ITECA News

**The Gender-Based Violence (GBV) Regulator publishes confidentiality guidance**
*Published: 2026-08-13 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Gender-Based Violence Regulator has released a Statement of Regulatory Expectations covering confidentiality, non-disclosure agreements, and compliance with the National Code of Practice for Providers of Education and Training to Overseas Students. The guidance is aimed at registered higher education providers, but CRICOS-registered RTOs that must comply with the National Code are also in scope. It clarifies expectations around how providers handle confidentiality arrangements in relation to GBV matters under the Code.
