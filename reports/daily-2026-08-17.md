### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Employer investment in worker skills is a critical contribution to Australia's future workforce**
*Published: 2026-08-14 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA's new Insights paper finds employer investment in workplace learning is a critical but often undercounted tool for workforce development. The paper highlights that structured and informal on-the-job training supplements formal VET, and calls for better measurement of employer training expenditure. RTOs may find this useful as evidence for why employer-funded training matters alongside government-subsidised pathways.

### Skills Insight

**Help shape future qualifications for the meat industry**
*Published: 2026-08-17 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Skills Insight is calling for industry input ahead of consultation workshops opening in September for two meat industry training product development projects. Draft materials will cover Certificate III meat processing qualifications, including boning, slaughtering, livestock handling, and packing. RTOs delivering these qualifications should register for workshops to contribute to the review and stay ahead of any changes to delivery requirements.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Meat industry cert reviews are open for comment — RTOs delivering these quals should register now before September workshops close.
