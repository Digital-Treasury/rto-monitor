**ASIC disqualifies New South Wales director Alan MacDonald for 5 years**
*Published: 2026-08-18 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Alan MacDonald of Bankstown, NSW has been disqualified from managing corporations for the maximum period of five years due to his involvement in the failure of three companies. ASIC's use of the maximum disqualification period signals strong enforcement of director duties against those who allow multiple corporate failures. For RTO owners who are also company directors, this is a practical reminder that ASIC pursues directors through company failure — not just deliberate misconduct.
