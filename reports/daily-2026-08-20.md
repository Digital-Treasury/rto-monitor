### NCVER

**Young Australians at 25: engaged in work and study, but facing greater challenges**
*Published: 2026-08-18 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

NCVER's latest LSAY infographic compares 25-year-olds across 2013, 2019 and 2025. While young Australians remain broadly engaged in work and study, the conditions have worsened: underemployment rose from 9% to 13%, financial stress doubled (18% to 37%), and the share reporting high psychological distress increased from 5% to 18%. Full-time employment among employed young people fell from 75% to 65%.

For RTOs training young people, this data is practical: student welfare and support frameworks built for a 2013 cohort are not calibrated for today's reality. RTOs with student support obligations under the Standards should review whether their services reflect these changed conditions.

Content-worthy: YES
