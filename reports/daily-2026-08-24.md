### ASIC Newsroom

**Get ready for new director ID requirements from 1 July 2027**
*Published: 2026-08-24 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has issued a reminder that new director identification requirements take effect from 1 July 2027. Directors are advised to check now that their company and personal information held by ASIC is current and accurate. For RTO owners operating as company directors, director ID compliance is a legal obligation that sits alongside fit-and-proper-person requirements under the Standards for RTOs — any lapse could have flow-on consequences for registration status.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: If you run an RTO as a company director, 1 July 2027 is the deadline for new ASIC director ID requirements — ASIC says check your details now.
