**Draft OSCA 2027 update**
*Published: 2026-08-27 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Australian Bureau of Statistics has published a draft Occupation Standard Classification for Australia (OSCA 2027) for public consultation, open until 18 September 2026. Skills Insight has identified 18 occupations across agriculture and food industries affected by proposed revisions. Changes to occupation classifications can flow through to training package structures — RTOs in affected industries should review and consider submitting feedback.

RTOs delivering agriculture training should review the drafts via the online Feedback Hub or email before the deadline.
