### ASQA Latest News

**ASQA's Corporate Plan 2026-27 published**
*Published: 2026-08-31 | Relevance: HIGH*

### Jobs and Skills Australia

**A national benchmark for VET outcomes**
*Published: 2026-08-28 | Relevance: HIGH*

Australia now has its first national baseline for measuring VET outcomes under the National Skills Agreement. The NSA Outcomes Framework Balanced Scorecard Baseline Report establishes system-wide indicators and targets, with specific coverage for priority cohorts including First Nations Australians, women and people with disability. Early findings show relative strengths in post-training earnings and employment outcomes, with enrolments in shortage occupations and national priority areas growing in recent years.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Australia's first national VET outcomes scorecard is published — these are the metrics governments will use to judge whether the training system is working.

**Total new vacancies rise 8% over the year**
*Published: 2026-08-28 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA's Total New Vacancies series estimates 1,353,000 new job vacancies in the May 2026 quarter, up 8% (around 99,000 vacancies) year-on-year. The series captures vacancies filled through all channels — including word of mouth, signage and social media — giving a broader picture of labour demand than online-only job ads. Useful context for RTOs planning enrolments in occupations with high vacancy rates.

### ITECA News

**National Update — Continuing Migration Policy By Confusion is Economic and Social Self-Harm**
*Published: 2026-08-31 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ITECA describes Australia's migration debate and policy responses as "shambolic," arguing that ongoing uncertainty is causing real economic and social harm. For RTOs with CRICOS registration, unclear migration settings directly complicate international student recruitment planning and partnership agreements with offshore agents. ITECA has flagged this as an ongoing advocacy priority.
