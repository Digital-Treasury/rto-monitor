### TEQSA News

**Annual reporting requirements for offshore courses published**
*Published: 2026-09-01 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

A new legislative instrument has been published specifying annual reporting requirements for providers of Australian courses of study delivered offshore. This follows amendments to the TEQSA Act in December 2025, which require providers to obtain TEQSA authorisation for offshore delivery, notify TEQSA of new or changed arrangements, and now submit an annual information report. The instrument applies to all registered higher education providers authorised under Part 3A of the TEQSA Act. Dual-sector RTOs with offshore delivery need to check their compliance obligations — a reporting deadline of 31 October 2026 was referenced in the instrument.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: If your RTO delivers courses offshore, mandatory annual reporting requirements are now in force — there's a 31 October deadline to be across.

URL: https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/annual-reporting-requirements-offshore-courses-published
