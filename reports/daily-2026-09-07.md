## New items

### ASQA Latest News

**Cancellation under ESOS Act of BSB80120 Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning)**
*Published: 2026-09-04 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Assistant Minister for International Education has made a legislative instrument specifying BSB80120 Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning) for automatic suspension and cancellation under the ESOS Act. Providers registered on CRICOS to deliver this course must immediately stop enrolling new international students; existing student obligations under the ESOS framework apply. This is a ministerial-level regulatory intervention, not an ASQA audit outcome.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: The government just cancelled a VET qualification under the ESOS Act — CRICOS-registered providers should audit their course schedules now. BSB80120 is gone.

### ITECA News

**User Choice Funding Round Now Open in the NT**
*Published: 2026-09-07 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Northern Territory government has opened an invitation for RTOs to apply for 2027 NT Apprenticeship and Traineeship (User Choice) funding. The round is open and contestable, running for four weeks and closing 5 October 2026. RTOs interested in delivering NT-funded apprenticeships or traineeships in 2027 must apply through this round — there is no second opportunity.

### ASIC Newsroom

**Former WA director Joanne Pellew sentenced to three and a half years imprisonment for Corporations Act offences**
*Published: 2026-09-07 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Joanne Pellew, a former Western Australian director, has been sentenced in the District Court of WA to 3.5 years imprisonment for dishonestly using her position as a director and for managing a corporation while disqualified. The offences relate to Corporations Act breaches applicable to any company director — including those running RTOs. This case reinforces that director duties carry real criminal exposure.
