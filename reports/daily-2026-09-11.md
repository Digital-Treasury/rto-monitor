### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC seeks feedback on future access to information on companies register**
*Published: 2026-09-11 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC released Consultation Paper 391 proposing changes to what information is available on the companies register and who can access it. From 1 July 2027, officeholders' names, status (current or ceased), director ID numbers, and whether a director has confirmed their director ID will be available to all registry users for free. Residential addresses and birth information would be restricted to verified business users only. For RTO owners and directors, the key practical implications are: (1) director ID confirmation status becomes publicly checkable; (2) residential addresses gain better protection from the general public. Submissions close date was not stated in the article — ASIC CP 391 should be checked directly for the consultation deadline.
