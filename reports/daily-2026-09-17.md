### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC remakes qualified accountant legislative instrument**
*Published: 2026-09-17 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has finalized and remade the legislative instrument that specifies which members of professional accounting bodies (CPA Australia, CA ANZ, IPA) are recognised as 'qualified accountants' under the Corporations Act. This matters for RTOs delivering accounting qualifications because graduates who join these professional bodies depend on this instrument to access roles — such as SMSF auditor, certain financial adviser functions, and insolvency-related positions — that require 'qualified accountant' status. The final instrument follows the consultation ASIC opened in July 2026.

### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Occupation Shortage Report now available as new-look Employer Activity Insights interactive table**
*Published: 2026-09-16 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Jobs and Skills Australia has replaced the static Occupation Shortage Report with a new interactive Employer Activity Insights table, allowing users to filter and explore occupation shortage data dynamically. RTOs use shortage data to prioritise which qualifications to invest in, apply for funded delivery contracts, and advise students on in-demand pathways. The shift to an interactive format makes the data more accessible but means any workflows relying on the old report format will need to be updated.
