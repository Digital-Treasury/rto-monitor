### ASQA Latest News

**Education agent commission reporting**
*Published: 2026-09-21 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Department of Education has announced the first education agent commissions data collection will open in PRISMS (Provider Registration and International Student Management System) on 21 September 2026. This is the inaugural collection under the agent commission transparency requirements introduced through the ESOS framework amendments. CRICOS-registered RTOs that use education agents to recruit international students are required to report agent commission data through PRISMS.
