### training.gov.au

**CPC Release 11.0 published 23/9/2026 — known bug: products incorrectly deleted**
*Published: 2026-09-23 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Construction, Plumbing and Services (CPC) Training Package Release 11.0 was published on 23 September 2026. Training.gov.au has flagged a known bug: some CPC products were incorrectly deleted from the release. Developers are investigating as a priority. There is also a separate bug affecting training package comparison tool emails, meaning RTOs may not have received automated change notifications.

RTOs delivering CPC qualifications should check their scope of registration, verify that the training products they deliver are still listed correctly, and hold off on finalising transition planning until the bug is resolved.
