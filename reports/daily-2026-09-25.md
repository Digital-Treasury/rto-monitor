### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Changing recruitment trends in the IT industry**
*Published: 2026-09-24 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The latest JSA Recruitment Experiences and Outlook Survey (REOS) Spotlight reveals a significant shift in Australia's IT labour market: employer concerns have moved from recruitment and retention difficulties to a lack of demand for products or services. The IT industry recruitment rate sits at 32%, well below the all-industry average of 49%, and only 16% of IT employers expect to increase staffing in the next quarter — the lowest level in recent years. The findings suggest IT skill mismatches and candidate oversupply may now be larger drivers than a shortage of applicants.

RTOs delivering IT qualifications (Certificate III in IT, Diploma of IT, Diploma of Systems Administration, etc.) should review their enrolment messaging — marketing these qualifications on the basis of a skills shortage may no longer reflect the current market, and student outcome expectations need calibrating.

Content-worthy: YES

### ASIC Newsroom

**Former Warwick Gold and Impact Gold director Yuetu Ma disqualified from managing corporations for four years**
*Published: 2026-09-25 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has disqualified former Queensland director Yuetu Ma (also known as Jack Ma) from managing corporations for four years, arising from his role in two failed companies: Warwick Gold Holdings Pty Ltd and Impact Gold Ltd. The disqualification follows a pattern of ASIC enforcement actions this year targeting directors of failed companies. RTO owners are company directors and carry equivalent legal obligations — repeated ASIC disqualification actions serve as reminders of the personal exposure directors face when a company fails under their watch.
