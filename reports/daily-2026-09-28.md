### training.gov.au

**25/9/2026 UPDATE — CPC Release 11.0 published 23/9/2026**
*Published: 2026-09-25 | Relevance: HIGH*

Four CPC (Construction, Plumbing and Construction) training package products — CPC31220, CPC40120, CPCCPB3014, CPCCPB3027 — were incorrectly deleted from the CPC training package when Release 11.0 was published on 23 September 2026. The issue has been resolved and the products are now current. RTOs delivering these qualifications or units should verify their scope of registration reflects the correct current status and check that any enrolments in affected products were not disrupted during the deletion window.

### ASQA Latest News

**ASQA Update — September 2026**
*Published: 2026-09-28 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASQA's September newsletter covers provider obligations to notify ASQA of material changes to their operations — an obligation RTOs are legally required to fulfil but commonly miss. The edition also covers the new ASQA–India NCVET Letter of Intent (supporting bilateral recognition of VET qualifications), the release of ASQA's AI principles for the VET sector, and ASQA's 2026-27 Corporate Plan. The material change notification piece is the most operationally significant item for compliance managers.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Is your RTO clear on what counts as a "material change" that must be reported to ASQA? The September Update spells it out — missing this obligation is a common compliance gap.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/728
