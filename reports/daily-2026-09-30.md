### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Fewer workers displaced, but job loss is shifting across Australia**
*Published: 2026-09-29 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA's new report "Recent trends for displaced workers in Australia" finds that overall displacement rates have declined over the past decade to 2025, but the industries, occupations and regions experiencing the most displacement have shifted significantly. Workers in some previously stable industries are now more exposed. The practical implication for RTOs: demand for retraining will come from different sectors than it has historically.

### NCVER News

**VET completion rates highlight diversity of learner pathways and outcomes**
*Published: 2026-09-29 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

NCVER's VET Qualification Completion Rates 2025 report finds that 48.9% of VET qualifications commenced in 2021 were completed within four years, down slightly from 49.5% for the 2020 cohort. Projected rates for 2022 and 2023 commencements are higher, suggesting improvement. The report emphasises that completion is not the only measure of learner success — many students exit with partial skills that lead to employment. Relevant benchmark for RTOs assessing their own completion performance against the national rate.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: 48.9% of VET qualifications are completed within four years. Is your RTO above or below the national benchmark — and does the data actually matter?
