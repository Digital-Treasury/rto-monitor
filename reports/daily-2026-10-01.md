### TEQSA News

**Consultation opens on draft SRE for adopting a definition of Islamophobia**
*Published: 2026-09-28 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA has opened public consultation on a draft Statement of Regulatory Expectations (SRE) for adopting a definition of Islamophobia, following a recent parallel consultation on antisemitism. The consultation closes 12 October 2026. Relevant to dual-sector RTOs that are also registered higher education providers under TEQSA. The SREs outline how providers are expected to demonstrate compliance with the Higher Education Standards Framework.
