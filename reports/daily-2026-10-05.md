## New items

### ASQA Latest News

**Extension of the overseas student transfer restriction period to 12 months**
*Published: 2026-10-02 | Relevance: HIGH*

From 2 October 2026, CRICOS providers are prohibited from enrolling a student seeking to transfer from another provider until that student has completed 12 months of their principal course — up from the previous 6-month restriction. The change is temporary, reverting to 6 months from 1 July 2027. It was made by the Assistant Minister for International Education under Standard 7 of the National Code, as part of broader student visa integrity reforms.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: CRICOS providers need to review enrolment intake procedures now — the transfer window doubled overnight from 6 to 12 months until July 2027, and enrolling a transfer student too early is a compliance breach.
