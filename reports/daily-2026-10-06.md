### ITECA News

**Nominations Open: 2026 International Student Voice Roundtable**
*Published: 2026-10-01 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Australian Government Department of Education is seeking nominations for the 2026 International Student Voice Roundtable, following the 2025 event with Assistant Minister for International Education, the Hon Julian Hill MP. The roundtable provides a direct channel for international student perspectives to inform government policy on international education. RTOs with CRICOS registration should consider whether their international students or staff should participate, as this is one of few formal mechanisms for the provider and student voice to reach ministerial level.
