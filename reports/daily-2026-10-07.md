### Jobs and Skills Australia

**New paper explores the state of adult learning in Australia**
*Published: 2026-10-06 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA's latest Lifelong Learning working paper, *Systems at work: Trends in adult learning and outcomes*, finds that unmet demand for adult learning is substantial and growing — many adults want to participate in VET or work-related training but don't. The paper shows positive outcomes (better employment, income growth, retention) when adults do participate, but flags that participation alone isn't enough unless skills are recognised and rewarded in the workplace. It calls for smoother pathways between VET, higher education, and employment.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: More adults want to upskill than actually do — use JSA's new data to show the gap and position your RTO's flexible delivery as the entry point they're looking for.
