**Ag trade apprenticeship qualification**
*Published: 2026-10-12 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Ag trade apprenticeship project has closed its broad consultation phase and is now in consensus gathering. Feedback from stakeholder engagement is being reviewed to refine the proposed qualification model. Online workshops are expected soon to allow feedback on updated draft training products before final submission. RTOs delivering agricultural apprenticeships should watch for workshop invitations and register to shape the final product.
