### Jobs and Skills Australia

**More than one way to find a skill: building a connected skills language for Australia**
*Published: 2026-10-08 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The second article in JSA's ongoing National Skills Taxonomy (NST) series, following their August 2026 piece on defining "skill" as human. This instalment addresses the practical challenge of organising thousands of skills so people, employers, and digital systems can find, compare and connect them across education, training and work. It outlines different approaches to skills language — including hierarchical taxonomies, cluster-based models and embedded-in-context skills — and argues Australia needs a connected skills language to make a skills-first system functional. For RTOs, this matters because how the national skills taxonomy is built will shape how qualifications are matched to job outcomes, how funding is distributed, and how RTO graduates are recognised by employers in a digitised labour market.

### TEQSA News

**Threshold Standard amendments and registration applications**
*Published: 2026-10-12 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA has published implementation guidance for the Higher Education Standards Framework Amendment (Threshold Standards) Instrument 2026, which was made in July 2026. The amendments cover two areas: racism standards (all providers must comply from 1 January 2027) and governance standards (universities from 1 January 2027; all other providers from 1 July 2027). The guidance specifically notes that dual-sector RTOs preparing registration or renewal applications must include evidence of how they will comply with the amended standards. This is directly relevant to dual-sector providers — those delivering both VET and higher education — who need to factor the new requirements into any upcoming TEQSA registration submissions.
