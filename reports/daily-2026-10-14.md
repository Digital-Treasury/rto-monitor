### training.gov.au

**Consultation on the Quality Indicator surveys summary is now open**
*Published: 2026-10-13 | Relevance: HIGH*

ASQA invites RTOs to provide feedback on drafts of the refreshed Quality Indicator Student Survey and Quality Indicator Employer Survey. Quality Indicators are a mandatory annual reporting requirement for all RTOs — every registered provider must collect learner engagement and employer satisfaction data using these surveys and submit results to the national register. Changes to the survey questions or structure will affect how RTOs collect and report this data. The consultation closes at an unspecified date; RTOs should review the draft surveys now to ensure their data collection processes remain compliant with any updates.

### ASIC Newsroom

**NSW concrete contractor Ali Youssef charged with dishonesty offences and dealing with proceeds of crime**
*Published: 2026-10-13 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has charged Ali Youssef, a director of Allscope Concrete & Pumping Pty Ltd, with dishonestly using his position as a company director and dealing with proceeds of crime. The matter appeared in the Sydney Downing Centre Local Court. RTO owners operating as company directors hold the same legal duties under the Corporations Act as directors of any other business — ASIC routinely pursues director misconduct across all sectors, not just financial services. This case is a reminder that director conduct obligations apply to RTO operators.
