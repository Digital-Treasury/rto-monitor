### ASIC Newsroom

**Former Rex director Lim Kim Hai disqualified for 4 years and ordered to pay $800,000 penalty for directors' duties and continuous disclosure contraventions**
*Published: 2026-10-20 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The NSW Supreme Court disqualified the former Rex Airlines CEO and executive chair from managing corporations for four years and ordered an $800,000 penalty for breaches of directors' duties and continuous disclosure obligations. RTO owners who are company directors face the same legal obligations — this outcome illustrates the personal financial and reputational consequences of directors' duties breaches at the executive level.
