### ASIC Newsroom

**Visy and Pratt companies pay over $4.1 million in infringement notices for failing to lodge financial reports on time**
*Published: 2026-10-21 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC issued infringement notices totalling $4.158 million to 21 companies in the Visy/Pratt group for allegedly failing to lodge their financial reports for the year ended 30 June 2025 on time. While the companies involved are large corporations, the enforcement action is a practical reminder that lodging annual financial reports on time is a legal requirement under the Corporations Act for all company directors — including those running RTOs. Failure to comply can attract substantial penalties.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: ASIC just fined 21 companies $4.1M for late financial report lodgements — RTO directors face the same obligation, and the Corporations Act doesn't have a "small business" exemption for late filing.
