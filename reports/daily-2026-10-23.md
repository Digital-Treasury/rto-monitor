### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC seeks leave to appeal NSW Supreme Court decision against former Rex non-executive directors**
*Published: 2026-10-23 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC is seeking leave to appeal the NSW Supreme Court decision that former Rex non-executive directors John Sharp, Siddharth Khotkar and Lincoln Pan did not breach their directors' duties in relation to a profit forecast made on 28 February 2023. The court found the directors were entitled to rely on management's representations when approving the forecast. ASIC's appeal challenges that standard of reliance — if successful, it would tighten the oversight obligations non-executive directors must exercise over management forecasts and disclosures. RTO owners who sit on boards or serve as non-executive directors should monitor this case; its outcome will clarify how much scrutiny directors are expected to apply to information from management before putting their name to it.

**Annual Report 2025-26 is available**
*Published: 2026-10-22 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA's Annual Report 2025-26 is now published, covering the 2025–26 financial year. Key themes include social cohesion, student safety, governance uplift (including the Expert Council on University Governance), and regulatory reform toward risk-based approaches. While TEQSA is the higher education regulator, dual-sector RTOs registered with both ASQA and TEQSA should review the report for changes to regulatory expectations that may affect their higher education operations.
