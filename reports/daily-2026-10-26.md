### ITECA News

**National Update - When the Market Stops Waiting**
*Published: 2026-10-26 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ITECA warns that more employers are bypassing the nationally recognised training system, opting instead for industry-endorsed programs outside the accredited system because they are faster, more agile, and more responsive to real workplace needs. ITECA cites a qualification still in use that was last updated 11 years ago as an example of system failure. The piece argues that if nationally accredited training remains slow and disconnected from employer needs, RTOs risk becoming the "slow, expensive sibling" employers use only when regulation compels them.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Employers are quietly opting out of nationally accredited training — ITECA's latest update explains why the VET system needs to move faster before it becomes irrelevant.

### NCVER News

**Government funding of VET 2025**
*Published: 2026-10-23 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

NCVER has published its annual Government Funding of VET statistical report for the 2025 calendar year. The report covers Australian and state/territory government funding across five categories: VET delivery funding, employer assistance, student assistance, capital expenditure, and system administration.
