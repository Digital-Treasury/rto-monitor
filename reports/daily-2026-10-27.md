### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Statement clarifying media references to '139,000 jobs lost to AI'**
*Published: 2026-10-27 | Relevance: HIGH*

JSA issued a clarification that its inaugural Jobs and Skills Report 2026 did not project 139,000 jobs would be lost to AI. The report actually modelled a shift in employment growth toward trade and technical occupations — not job losses. Media outlets misrepresented the finding. The report frames AI as driving demand for skilled tradespeople, not replacing them.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: The media got it wrong. JSA's own report says AI is pushing MORE demand toward trade and technical training — not less. Here's what the data actually shows.
