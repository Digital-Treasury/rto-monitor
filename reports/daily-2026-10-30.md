### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC begins further review of financial adviser qualifications**
*Published: 2026-10-30 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has commenced a further review of the Financial Advisers Register (FAR) to verify whether relevant providers have met the qualifications standard. This follows ASIC's earlier update on compliance with the financial adviser qualifications standard. RTOs that deliver FASEA-aligned qualifications or courses used by financial advisers to meet the education standard should ensure their graduates are correctly recorded in the FAR and that course completion documentation is in order.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: ASIC is actively checking the financial adviser qualifications register again — RTOs delivering financial planning courses need to confirm graduate data is accurate and complete.

**Hiring difficulties persist as recruitment eases**
*Published: 2026-10-29 | Relevance: MEDIUM*
