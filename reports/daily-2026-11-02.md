### TEQSA News

**TEQSA publishes SRE on adopting a definition of antisemitism**
*Published: 2026-10-29 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA has published a Statement of Regulatory Expectations (SRE) requiring higher education providers to implement a definition of antisemitism by 1 January 2027. The SRE clarifies that providers must integrate their adopted definition into complaints processes, staff/student guidance and institutional decision-making, while preserving academic freedom protections under the Threshold Standards. Dual-sector providers — RTOs that also hold TEQSA registration — need to act on this now to meet the January deadline.

### Jobs and Skills Australia

**Webinar: More than one way to find a skill**
*Published: 2026-11-02 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA has published the recording of the second National Skills Taxonomy (NST) webinar, in which Deputy Commissioner Megan Lilly introduced the concept of a skills polyhierarchy — organising skills so they are discoverable through multiple pathways rather than rigid categories. The NST will shape how qualifications and units of competency are structured in future training package reviews. RTOs involved in training package development or curriculum planning should watch this to understand how skills classification is changing.

### ASIC Newsroom

**Former Youpla Group CEO Bryn Jones and director Ronald Pattenden found to have breached their duties as directors**
*Published: 2026-10-30 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

A Federal Court finding confirmed that the CEO and a director of the Youpla Group (an Aboriginal funeral insurance company) breached their duties as directors. The case reinforces ASIC's active enforcement posture on director conduct — specifically failure to act in good faith and in the interests of the company. For RTO owners who are also company directors, this adds to a string of recent ASIC actions making clear that director obligations are taken seriously regardless of company size or sector.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Another ASIC director duties finding — what RTO owners need to know about their personal liability as company directors.

### ITECA News

**National Update — We need to invest in students, not institutions**
*Published: date unclear (appears as most recent item on ITECA news listing as of 2026-11-02) | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ITECA examines what it describes as a structural imbalance in VET funding, arguing that public skills training investment is too often captured by institutional interests rather than flowing to student outcomes and value for taxpayers. This is a recurring policy-direction theme for ITECA and reflects ongoing debate about how the National Skills Agreement funding criteria are applied. Relevant context for RTOs navigating funding competition with public providers.
