### ASIC Newsroom

**ASIC issues over $4.3 million in infringement notices to 22 companies for alleged failure to lodge financial reports**
*Published: 2026-11-04 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC issued infringement notices totalling over $4.3 million to 22 large proprietary companies as part of ongoing financial reporting compliance surveillance. The notices target companies that failed to lodge financial reports on time. ASIC has been systematically pursuing reporting failures as part of its small business and corporate governance enforcement focus.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: RTO operators running companies are subject to the same financial reporting obligations — this enforcement wave is a direct reminder that ASIC is actively pursuing late lodgements, with penalties now exceeding $200,000 per company.

**Federal Court upholds $11 million in penalties and disqualification against iSignthis and former director and CEO Nickolas John Karantzis**
*Published: 2026-11-03 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Federal Court upheld $11 million in penalties and a director disqualification against iSignthis (now Southern Cross Payments) and its former CEO, affirming both the liability finding and the penalty on appeal. The case reinforces that directors face personal liability for financial misconduct and cannot rely on appeals to escape enforcement outcomes. RTO owners who are company directors face the same legal framework.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Directors who think an appeal will soften ASIC enforcement outcomes should read this — the Federal Court just confirmed the full $11M penalty and disqualification, sending a clear signal about personal director accountability.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-267mr-federal-court-upholds-11-million-in-penalties-and-disqualification-against-isignthis-and-former-director-and-ceo-nickolas-john-karantzis
