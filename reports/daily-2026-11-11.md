### ASIC Newsroom

**Former Youpla Group executives found to have breached their directors' duties**
*Published: 2026-11-11 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Federal Court found former Youpla Group board members Jonathan Law, Michael Wilson and Geoffrey Clayton breached their duties as directors across multiple Youpla Group companies. All three have been disqualified from managing corporations and ordered to pay pecuniary penalties. Youpla Group operated Aboriginal funeral funds that collapsed in 2022.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: Another Federal Court directors' duties case — if you're a director of an RTO, this is a reminder that ASIC's enforcement of director obligations is ongoing and consequential.

**ASIC review highlights how small business restructuring can help businesses experiencing financial difficulty**
*Published: 2026-11-11 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has published a new report examining five years of the small business restructuring (SBR) process. The report outlines how eligible small businesses (under $1M in debt) can restructure while remaining in control, as an alternative to voluntary administration. For RTO owners managing cash flow pressures, this is a useful reference on a legal pathway that is less severe than administration.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: ASIC's 5-year review of small business restructuring — if your RTO is under financial pressure, there's a formal pathway that lets you stay in control. Worth knowing before it becomes urgent.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-277mr-asic-review-highlights-how-small-business-restructuring-can-help-businesses-experiencing-financial-difficulty
