### ITECA News

**Join Session 2 of the FSO AI action learning project: AI for VET admin and compliance**
*Published: 2026-11-12 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Future Skills Organisation (FSO) is running Session 2 of its AI action learning project specifically for VET teachers, trainers, assessors, and administrative and compliance staff. This follows the discovery workshop in October (Session 1). Session 2 focuses on validation and prioritisation of AI use cases in VET admin and compliance settings. This is a free, structured opportunity for RTO staff to engage with AI tools in a practical compliance context — directly relevant to reducing administrative burden and improving compliance outcomes.
