**ASIC bans former ISG Financial Services Limited director Benjamin Godfrey from managing corporations for 5 years**
*Published: 2026-11-17 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has banned Benjamin Godfrey, former director and responsible manager of ISG Financial Services Limited and seven related companies, from managing corporations for five years following the failure of those companies. The ban was imposed due to his involvement in company failures, consistent with ASIC's ongoing enforcement of director duties under the Corporations Act. RTO owners who are company directors face the same legal obligations — this case is a reminder that ASIC actively enforces director responsibilities across all sectors, not just financial services.
