### TEQSA News

**Revised Transnational Education Toolkit published**
*Published: 2026-11-18 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA has updated its Transnational Education Toolkit (originally published 2022), providing a structured framework for providers delivering Australian higher education awards offshore. The revised toolkit is organised around the TNE lifecycle and includes two new implementation guidance sections covering partnership establishment and applied governance arrangements. Relevant to CRICOS-registered RTOs with offshore delivery partnerships — TEQSA is signalling it expects providers to self-assure against this updated framework.

### Skills Insight

**Year in Review 2025-26: Building a stronger skills system, together**
*Published: 2026-11-19 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Skills Insight (the Jobs and Skills Council for agriculture and related industries) has published its 2025-26 annual review, reporting 27 completed projects across 13 industries including agriculture, food processing, horticulture, textiles, meat processing, veterinary nursing, and racing. The organisation conducted over 19,500 formal engagements and contributed to 10 national policy discussions. For RTOs delivering in these industries, the high volume of completed projects signals that training package updates and revised qualifications are likely to flow through the system in the coming 6–12 months.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: 27 industry skills projects wrapped up in 2025-26 by Skills Insight — if you deliver training in ag, food processing, textiles or vet nursing, here's your signal to check which qualifications are in the update pipeline.
