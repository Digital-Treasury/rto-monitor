### ASIC Newsroom

**Berndale director Stavro D'Amore refused leave to appeal jail sentence**
*Published: 2026-11-23 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The Full Federal Court refused former Berndale Capital Securities director Stavro D'Amore's application for leave to appeal his jail sentence for multiple dishonesty offences. The original sentence stands. Berndale was a financial services firm, but the case reinforces that directors cannot escape criminal accountability through repeated appeals. RTO operators who are company directors face the same obligations and consequences under the Corporations Act.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: If you're a director of an RTO (or any company), this Full Federal Court decision is worth five minutes of your time — it's a reminder that dishonesty offences don't disappear on appeal.

**Former NSW director Philip Iacovou charged with dishonestly using his position**
*Published: 2026-11-23 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Philip Iacovou has been charged with using his position as director of Outer Inner Property Solutions Pty Ltd (in liquidation) dishonestly, intending to gain an advantage for himself or someone else. This is a standard s184 Corporations Act charge (criminal misuse of director position). RTO owners who are directors of their operating entity need to understand this obligation — benefiting personally at the company's expense is a criminal offence, not just a civil one.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: RTO operators: your director duties are personal and criminal, not just regulatory. The latest ASIC charge out of NSW is a timely reminder.
