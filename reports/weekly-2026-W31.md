## Lead items this week

### 1. ASQA releases AI principles for VET providers
**Source:** ASQA Latest News | **Date:** 30 Jul 2026 | **Relevance:** HIGH

ASQA released 5 Principles for the Responsible Use of AI in VET, covering all aspects of a provider's operations. No new regulatory requirements — the framework maps AI obligations to existing standards under the 2025 Standards for RTOs — but it signals the lens ASQA auditors will apply when AI-related questions arise. Includes self-assurance questions and case studies. All RTOs using AI tools in delivery, assessment, or operations should review this now.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/718

### 2. NCVER: Full VET qualification enrolments fell 5% in 2025
**Source:** NCVER | **Date:** 22 Jul 2026 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

NCVER's Total VET Students and Courses 2025 report shows 5.1 million participants — participation near record levels — but full qualification enrolments dropped more than 5%, with every jurisdiction recording declines. Certificate III and IV hit hardest; government-funded programs most affected. Short courses and standalone subjects grew, especially in fee-for-service. This is the key annual benchmark for understanding where the market is heading.

URL: https://www.ncver.edu.au/news-and-events/media-releases/vet-participation-remains-high,-while-qualification-enrolments-fall

### 3. NCVER: Trade apprentice completions up; non-trade declining for third consecutive cohort
**Source:** NCVER | **Date:** 28 Jul 2026 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

Trade apprentice completion rates reached 58.7% for the 2019 commencing cohort, with electricians at 71.7% and carpenters/joiners at 63.7%. Non-trade and traineeship completions declined for a third consecutive cohort. Apprentices with disability recorded some of the lowest rates. RTOs delivering non-trade programs should treat this as a systemic retention signal, not an outlier.

URL: https://www.ncver.edu.au/news-and-events/media-releases/trade-apprentices-completing-at-higher-rates

### 4. ASIC director enforcement — two cases this week
**Source:** ASIC Newsroom | **Dates:** 24 & 30 Jul 2026 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

Two director enforcement actions this week are relevant to RTO owners as company directors. Former construction director Vickie Vella was sentenced for using $1.2 million in company funds for personal use; Larry Dawson was permanently disqualified after facilitating a scheme that defrauded investors of ~$7 million while serving as sole director. Both illustrate active ASIC enforcement of directors' duties across all sectors, not just financial services.

- https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-169mr-former-construction-industry-director-vickie-vella-sentenced-after-using-1-2-million-in-company-money-for-personal-use/
- https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-174mr-federal-court-imposes-permanent-director-s-disqualification-order-against-larry-dawson/

### 5. Training package updates: meat processing and retail butchery under review
**Source:** Skills Insight | **Date:** 28 Jul 2026 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

Skills Insight launched a review of Certificate III meat processing qualifications (boning, slaughtering, livestock handling, food services, packing) and released a Workforce Functional Analysis for the Retail Butchery and Smallgoods review, signalling qualification changes are imminent. RTOs delivering in either area should register for consultation.
