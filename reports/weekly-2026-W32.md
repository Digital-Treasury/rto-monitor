### 1. ASQA and TEQSA sign MOU for dual-sector providers
*Published: 2026-08-05 | Source: ASQA Latest News | Relevance: HIGH*

ASQA and TEQSA have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to coordinate regulatory oversight for dual-sector providers registered with both agencies. The MOU establishes a Joint Strategic Oversight Committee, aims to align evidence requirements and audit processes, and is designed to reduce duplicate reporting obligations. Dual-sector providers should expect joint guidance materials and more coordinated audit scheduling in coming months — practical changes to how they interact with both regulators.

### 3. ASIC disqualifies Victorian director for 5 years across 8 company failures
*Published: 2026-08-04 | Source: ASIC Newsroom | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC has disqualified Melbourne-based Antonio Torcasio from managing corporations for five years, citing his involvement in the failure of eight companies involving insolvent trading, failure to maintain books/records, and lodgement breaches. RTO owners who hold directorships across multiple entities should note that a pattern of company failures triggers ASIC scrutiny irrespective of which industry is involved.
