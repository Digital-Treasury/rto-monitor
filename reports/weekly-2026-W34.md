### 1. Meat industry qualification review open for consultation — deadline September

**Source:** Skills Insight | **Published:** 2026-08-17 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

Skills Insight has opened registration for consultation workshops on two meat processing qualification reviews, with draft materials available in September. ### 2. ASIC disqualifies NSW director for 5 years after 3 company failures

**Source:** ASIC Newsroom | **Published:** 2026-08-18 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

Alan MacDonald of Bankstown, NSW was disqualified from managing corporations for five years due to his involvement in the failure of three companies. ASIC applied the maximum disqualification period, signalling that involvement across multiple failed companies — not just deliberate misconduct — is sufficient for the harshest available outcome. RTO owners who are company directors should note that ASIC enforcement runs independently of ASQA; Standards compliance does not shield a director from Corporations Act liability.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-196mr-asic-disqualifies-new-south-wales-director-alan-macdonald-for-5-years/

### 3. Young Australians at 25 are under greater financial stress — implications for student support

**Source:** NCVER | **Published:** 2026-08-18 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

NCVER's longitudinal data tracking Australians to age 25 shows sharp deterioration in financial and mental health conditions: underemployment up from 9% to 13%, financial stress up from 18% to 37%, and psychological distress up from 5% to 18% since 2013. Full-time employment among employed young people fell from 75% to 65%. For RTOs training young people, student support frameworks built for pre-pandemic conditions are calibrated to a different cohort than the one enrolling now. RTOs with support obligations under the Standards for RTOs should check whether their services reflect the changed reality.

URL: https://www.ncver.edu.au/news-and-events/media-releases/young-australians-at-25-engaged-in-work-and-study,-but-facing-greater-challenges

### 4. JSA employer investment papers: workplace learning undercounts VET

**Source:** Jobs and Skills Australia | **Published:** 2026-08-14 | **Relevance:** MEDIUM

JSA's new Insights paper and accompanying media release argue that employer-funded workplace learning is a significant but systematically undermeasured contributor to workforce development — formal VET enrolments don't capture it. The paper calls for better measurement of employer training expenditure and positions on-the-job training as complementary to, not competing with, formal qualifications. For RTOs, this is useful evidence when making the case for employer-funded training partnerships.

URLs:
- https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/employer-investment-worker-skills-critical-contribution-australias-future-workforce
- https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/new-insights-paper-explores-employer-investment-learning
