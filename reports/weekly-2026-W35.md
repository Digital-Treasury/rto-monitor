## Lead items

**1. ASQA's 2026-27 Regulatory Risk Priorities published** *(HIGH)*
ASQA's August IQ newsletter (25 Aug) released the 2026-27 Regulatory Risk Priorities alongside AI principles for VET and a reminder that Data Provision Requirements amendments take effect 1 October 2026. The Risk Priorities document is the clearest signal of where ASQA will focus audit scrutiny in the coming year. Every RTO should read it before its next self-assurance cycle. The 1 October DPR deadline is an imminent compliance date.
URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/722

**2. Skills Insight Workforce Plan 2026-27 launched** *(HIGH)*
Launched by the Minister for Skills and Training on 27 Aug, this plan maps skills needs and training priorities across agriculture, food production, and essential industries. RTOs in these sectors should use it to anticipate which qualifications will attract development focus and funding attention over the next 12–18 months.
URL: https://skillsinsight.com.au/news/workforce-plan-2026-2027-launch/

**3. Rural Operations qualification nearing finalisation** *(HIGH)*
The updated Certificate II in Primary Industries and Rural Operations has cleared its public feedback stage and is progressing to final checks. RTOs delivering this qualification should begin transition planning now.
URL: https://skillsinsight.com.au/news/rural-operations-project-moving-to-finalisation/

**4. Ag Trade Apprenticeship feedback — deadline 31 August** *(MEDIUM — URGENT)*
Consultation on the draft Agricultural Trade Qualification closes Sunday 31 August. RTOs delivering agriculture training have a narrow window to submit feedback via the Skills Insight Feedback Hub or email.
URL: https://skillsinsight.com.au/news/reminder-ag-trade-apprenticeship-drafts-available-for-feedback/

**5. ASIC: new director ID requirements from 1 July 2027** *(MEDIUM)*
ASIC issued a reminder (24 Aug) that new director identification requirements take effect 1 July 2027. RTO owners operating as company directors should verify their ASIC records are current. Director ID compliance sits alongside fit-and-proper-person requirements under the Standards for RTOs.
URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/get-ready-for-new-director-id-requirements-from-1-july-2027
