## Top stories this week

### 1. ASQA's Corporate Plan 2026-27 signals ongoing enforcement focus

ASQA released its Corporate Plan 2026-27 on 31 August. CEO Lisa Rice emphasises that the 2025 Standards for RTOs — in effect since 1 July 2025 — represent the most significant VET regulatory change in a generation, marking a shift from prescriptive compliance to outcomes-focused quality assurance. The Integrity Unit will continue targeting non-genuine providers and fraudulent qualification issuers in partnership with law enforcement.

**Why it matters:** RTOs should read this document as a roadmap for where ASQA will focus audit and enforcement attention over the next four years. Outcomes-focused compliance means demonstrating genuine student achievement, not just ticking procedural boxes.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/724

### 2. Australia's first national VET outcomes benchmark published

Jobs and Skills Australia released the NSA Outcomes Framework Balanced Scorecard Baseline Report on 28 August. This is the first system-wide benchmark for measuring VET outcomes under the National Skills Agreement. Key indicators include post-training employment, earnings, qualification completion rates, and enrolments in shortage occupations. Priority cohorts tracked include First Nations Australians, women, and people with disability.

**Why it matters:** These are now the metrics by which the federal government will assess whether VET funding is working. RTOs whose student cohorts perform well on these indicators may see competitive advantages in state-funded contracts and Skills First arrangements going forward.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/national-benchmark-vet-outcomes

### 3. New annual reporting obligation for providers delivering courses offshore

A legislative instrument published on 1 September specifies annual reporting requirements for providers authorised to deliver Australian courses of study offshore under Part 3A of the TEQSA Act. Providers must now submit an annual information report to TEQSA. A deadline of 31 October 2026 was referenced in the instrument.

**Why it matters:** Dual-sector RTOs (CRICOS-registered with TEQSA authorisation) delivering any offshore programs need to check whether the annual reporting obligation applies and put a process in place before October.

URL: https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/annual-reporting-requirements-offshore-courses-published

### 4. Training package consultation wave: meat processing, racing/breeding, landscaping

Skills Insight opened consultation on multiple qualifications this week:
- **Meat processing** (Sep 1): Drafts open for comment — Certificate III in Meat Processing significantly updated, aligned to the Training Package Organising Framework 2025.
- **Racing and breeding** (Aug 28): Seeking industry input to define the purpose and structure of qualifications.
- **Landscaping** (Aug 27): Consultation workshops open to confirm job roles, career pathways and technical skills.

**Why it matters:** RTOs delivering in these sectors should review open drafts and submit feedback. Industry input during this stage directly shapes the units and qualifications they will be required to deliver. Deadlines vary — check Skills Insight project pages.
