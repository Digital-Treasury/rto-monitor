## Top items this week

### 1. BSB80120 Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning) cancelled under ESOS Act
*Source: ASQA Latest News | Published: 2026-09-04 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Assistant Minister for International Education made a legislative instrument specifying BSB80120 Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning) for automatic suspension and cancellation under the ESOS Act. CRICOS-registered providers must stop enrolling new international students in this course immediately. Existing student obligations under the ESOS framework remain in force. This is a ministerial-level regulatory action, not an audit outcome.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/725

### 2. NT User Choice 2027 Funding Round — closes 5 October 2026
*Source: ITECA News | Published: 2026-09-07 | Relevance: HIGH*

The Northern Territory government opened an open and contestable funding round for RTOs to apply for 2027 NT Apprenticeship and Traineeship (User Choice) funding. The round runs for four weeks and closes 5 October 2026. No second round is offered; RTOs interested in NT-funded delivery in 2027 must apply now.

URL: https://www.iteca.edu.au/web/news/Articles/2026/User%20Choice%20Funding%20Round%20Now%20Open%20in%20the%20NT.aspx

### 3. Former WA director sentenced to 3.5 years imprisonment for Corporations Act offences
*Source: ASIC Newsroom | Published: 2026-09-07 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

Joanne Pellew was sentenced in the District Court of WA to 3.5 years imprisonment for dishonestly using her director position and managing a corporation while disqualified. These are Corporations Act offences that apply to any company director, including RTO owners. The case reinforces that director duties carry real criminal exposure — not just civil penalties.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-208mr-former-wa-director-joanne-pellew-sentenced-to-three-and-a-half-years-imprisonment-for-corporations-act-offences

### 4. TEQSA consultation on antisemitism Statement of Regulatory Expectations — closes 17 September
*Source: TEQSA News | Published: 2026-09-03 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

TEQSA is consulting on a draft Statement of Regulatory Expectations (SRE) on adopting a definition of antisemitism. Submissions close 5pm AEST 17 September 2026. Applicable primarily to dual-sector providers and RTOs delivering higher education courses; VET-only providers are not directly affected.

URL: https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/consultation-opens-draft-statement-regulatory-expectations

### 5. ASIC proposes publicly visible Director ID confirmation status from July 2027
*Source: ASIC Newsroom | Published: 2026-09-11 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC Consultation Paper 391 proposes that from 1 July 2027, all registry users can see whether a director has confirmed their Director ID. Director names, status, and ID numbers would be freely available. Residential addresses and birth information would be restricted to verified business users. For RTO directors, the practical issue is: unconfirmed Director IDs will become publicly visible. Consultation is currently open.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/asic-seeks-feedback-on-future-access-to-information-on-companies-register
