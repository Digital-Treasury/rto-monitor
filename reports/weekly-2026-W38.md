## Top items this week

### 1. ASIC companies register: director ID confirmation goes public from July 2027
*Source: ASIC Newsroom | Published: 2026-09-11 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC released Consultation Paper 391 proposing that from 1 July 2027, the companies register will publicly display whether a director has confirmed their Director ID. Officeholders' names, current/ceased status, and director ID confirmation status would be visible to all registry users for free. Residential addresses and birth details would be restricted to verified business users. For RTO owners and directors, the practical implication is clear: any director who hasn't confirmed their Director ID will have that gap visible on the public register. Submissions close on CP 391 — check the ASIC website for the deadline.

**Content angle:** From July 2027, ASIC's register will publicly show if a director hasn't confirmed their Director ID. If yours isn't confirmed, that'll be visible too. Check and action it now.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/asic-seeks-feedback-on-future-access-to-information-on-companies-register

### 2. ASIC finalises qualified accountant legislative instrument
*Source: ASIC Newsroom | Published: 2026-09-17 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

ASIC remade the legislative instrument specifying which members of CPA Australia, CA ANZ, and the IPA are recognised as 'qualified accountants' under the Corporations Act. This closes the loop on the July 2026 consultation. For RTOs delivering accounting qualifications, the instrument determines which professional memberships unlock restricted roles for graduates — including SMSF auditor, certain financial adviser functions, and insolvency-related positions. No substantive changes from the draft were signalled.

**Content angle:** RTOs delivering accounting qualifications — ASIC's finalised 'qualified accountant' instrument confirms which professional memberships unlock restricted roles for your graduates.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/asic-remakes-qualified-accountant-legislative-instrument

### 3. Jobs and Skills Australia replaces Occupation Shortage Report with interactive tool
*Source: Jobs and Skills Australia | Published: 2026-09-16 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

The static Occupation Shortage Report has been replaced by the new Employer Activity Insights interactive table. RTOs use shortage data to prioritise qualification investment, apply for funded delivery contracts, and advise students on in-demand career paths. The shift to a dynamic interactive format makes occupation-level filtering and comparison easier but means any processes relying on the old report format need to be updated.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/occupation-shortage-report-now-available-new-look-employer-activity-insights-interactive-table
