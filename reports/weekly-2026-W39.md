## Most significant items

**1. CPC Training Package Release 11.0 — known bug: products incorrectly deleted**
*24 September 2026 | HIGH | training.gov.au*

Construction, Plumbing and Services (CPC) Training Package Release 11.0 was published on 23 September with a known defect: some CPC products were incorrectly deleted from the release. Training.gov.au has also flagged a separate bug affecting automated change notification emails, meaning RTOs may not have received the usual alerts. RTOs with construction scope should verify their training products are still listed correctly before making any transition decisions.

URL: https://training.gov.au

**2. Education agent commission reporting opens in PRISMS**
*21 September 2026 | HIGH | ASQA Latest News*

The inaugural education agent commission data collection opened on 21 September 2026 in PRISMS (Provider Registration and International Student Management System). This is the first collection under ESOS framework amendments requiring transparency around agent commissions. CRICOS-registered RTOs that use agents to recruit international students are required to submit commission data. This is a new compliance obligation — action required now.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/727

---

**3. IT sector hiring falls sharply — only 16% of IT employers plan to hire**
*24 September 2026 | MEDIUM | Jobs and Skills Australia*

JSA data shows Australia's IT labour market has shifted fundamentally: employers are now more concerned about lack of demand for their products than they are about finding staff. The IT recruitment rate (32%) is well below the all-industry average (49%), and only 16% of IT employers expect to increase staffing in the next quarter. RTOs delivering IT qualifications should review whether their marketing accurately reflects the current market.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/changing-recruitment-trends-it-industry

**4. Retail butchery and smallgoods training package drafts open for feedback**
*23 September 2026 | MEDIUM | Skills Insight*

Draft qualifications, skill sets and units of competency for retail butchery and smallgoods are open for stakeholder feedback until 31 October 2026. RTOs delivering food sector qualifications should review the drafts and engage in workshops. Consultation window closes 31 October — one week into the next term.

URL: https://skillsinsight.com.au/news/retail-butchery-and-smallgoods-project/
