## This week's most significant items

### 1. Overseas student transfer restriction extended to 12 months

*Source: ASQA Latest News | Date: 2 October 2026 | Relevance: HIGH*

From 2 October 2026, CRICOS providers must wait 12 months — up from 6 months — before enrolling a student transferring from another provider. The change is temporary under Standard 7 of the National Code and reverts to 6 months from 1 July 2027. The decision was made by the Assistant Minister for International Education as part of broader international student visa integrity reforms.

**Action required:** CRICOS providers should update enrolment procedures and staff training immediately to reflect the new 12-month restriction. Enrolling a transfer student before the window lapses is a direct Standards compliance breach.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: CRICOS providers: the transfer restriction just doubled to 12 months — here's what that changes in your enrolment process, and what happens if you get it wrong.

### 2. JSA paper: substantial unmet demand for adult VET participation

*Source: Jobs and Skills Australia | Date: 6 October 2026 | Relevance: MEDIUM*

JSA's new *Systems at work: Trends in adult learning and outcomes* paper finds that unmet demand for adult VET is large and growing. Adults who participate see better employment outcomes, but many who want to train don't — due to cost, access, and workplace culture barriers. The paper calls for smoother pathways between VET and employment, and for skills to be formally recognised and rewarded at work.

Content-worthy: YES
LinkedIn angle: More adults want to upskill than actually do — JSA's new data quantifies the gap and gives RTOs a strong argument for flexible, accessible delivery.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/new-paper-explores-state-adult-learning-australia
