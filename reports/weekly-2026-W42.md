## Top items this week

### 1. Quality Indicator survey consultation open — mandatory reporting affected
*HIGH | training.gov.au | 14 October 2026*

ASQA has opened consultation on drafts of the refreshed Quality Indicator Student Survey and Employer Survey. Quality Indicators are mandatory annual reporting requirements for every registered RTO. Any changes to survey structure or questions will flow directly into how RTOs collect and report data. RTOs should review the draft surveys now. No closing date specified — act early.

URL: https://training.gov.au/news/consultation-quality-indicator-surveys-summary-now-open

### 2. National skills taxonomy — shaping how qualifications are valued
*MEDIUM | Jobs and Skills Australia | 13 October 2026*

JSA's National Skills Taxonomy series continues with an article on how Australia plans to organise skills so people, employers, and digital systems can find and connect them. How this taxonomy is built will shape how VET qualifications are matched to jobs, how funding is allocated, and how RTO graduates are recognised in a digitised labour market. Worth tracking for RTO leaders thinking about qualification positioning.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/insights/more-one-way-find-skill-building-connected-skills-language-australia

### 3. TEQSA Threshold Standards implementation guidance published
*MEDIUM | TEQSA | 13 October 2026*

Implementation guidance now available for the Higher Education Standards Framework amendments made in July 2026 (racism and governance standards). Compliance dates: racism standards — all providers from 1 January 2027; governance standards — all non-university providers from 1 July 2027. Dual-sector RTOs preparing registration or renewal applications with TEQSA must include evidence of compliance.

URL: https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/threshold-standard-amendments-and-registration-applications
