## Lead items — highest significance

### 1. ASQA Version 2 Practice Guides released (HIGH)
*Published: 2026-10-29 | Source: ASQA Latest News*

ASQA released the second version of its Practice Guides for the 2025 Standards for RTOs. These are ASQA's principal practical resource explaining compliant practice under the 2025 Standards, updated based on provider feedback and questions since the first release. For RTOs still working through 2025 Standards implementation, this is the single most actionable resource published this week.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/3050

---

### 2. JSA clarifies AI and jobs: trade skills demand is rising (HIGH)
*Published: 2026-10-27 | Source: Jobs and Skills Australia*

JSA issued a formal statement correcting media misrepresentation of its Jobs and Skills Report 2026. The report did not project 139,000 jobs would be lost to AI — it modelled a shift of employment growth toward trade and technical occupations. RTOs can use this clarification to push back against client or student concern about AI displacing trade careers, and as a positive narrative for enrolment messaging.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/statement-clarifying-media-references-139000-jobs-lost-ai

### 3. ASQA Annual Report 2025-26 published (MEDIUM)
*Published: 2026-10-23 | Source: ASQA Latest News*

ASQA tabled its 2025-26 Annual Report in Parliament. Key themes: support for providers implementing the 2025 Standards, strengthened action on qualification integrity threats, and an intelligence-led risk-based regulatory approach. The report's enforcement data and stated priorities signal what ASQA will be watching in the year ahead.

URL: https://www.asqa.gov.au/node/3051

### 4. ASIC begins new review of financial adviser qualifications (MEDIUM)
*Published: 2026-10-30 | Source: ASIC Newsroom*

ASIC commenced a further review of the Financial Advisers Register to verify provider compliance with the qualifications standard. RTOs delivering FASEA-aligned courses or financial adviser qualifications should confirm graduate records on the FAR are accurate.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/asic-begins-further-review-of-financial-adviser-qualifications

---

### 5. ITECA: Employers quietly opting out of nationally accredited training (MEDIUM)
*Published: 2026-10-26 | Source: ITECA News*

ITECA warned that employers are increasingly bypassing nationally recognised training for faster industry-endorsed alternatives, citing qualifications unchanged for over a decade as a driver. The piece argues RTOs risk becoming the option employers use only when regulation requires it, unless the sector accelerates responsiveness.

URL: https://www.iteca.edu.au/web/news/Articles/2026/National_Update%20-%20When%20the%20Market%20Stops%20Waiting.aspx
