## Lead items this week

### 1. ASIC enforcement: Youpla Group directors disqualified and penalised
*Source: ASIC Newsroom | 11 November 2026 | MEDIUM*

The Federal Court found three former Youpla Group executives — Jonathan Law, Michael Wilson and Geoffrey Clayton — breached their directors' duties. All three were disqualified from managing corporations and ordered to pay pecuniary penalties. This follows the earlier finding against Youpla CEO Bryn Jones and director Ronald Pattenden (2 November). Youpla operated Aboriginal funeral funds that collapsed in 2022.

The pattern of enforcement is relevant for all RTO owners as company directors: ASIC is actively pursuing director accountability, and the range of sectors involved — from funeral funds to construction — signals no sector is exempt.

URL: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-278mr-former-youpla-group-executives-jonathan-law-michael-wilson-and-geoffrey-clayton-found-to-have-breached-their-directors-duties

### 2. JSA frames lifelong learning as essential to Australia's labour market
*Source: Jobs and Skills Australia | 6 November 2026 | MEDIUM*

Jobs and Skills Australia published a media release and companion report arguing Australia's labour market resilience depends on building, using and growing capabilities across people's working lives. The lifelong learning framing positions ongoing VET participation as national infrastructure — not a fallback option. For RTOs delivering short courses, upskilling and reskilling programs, this is a policy validation from the peak workforce advisory body.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/australias-future-labour-market-depends-positive-approach-lifelong-learning

### 3. JSA Regional Roadmap: strong demand, persistent barriers in regional Australia
*Source: Jobs and Skills Australia | 10 November 2026 | MEDIUM*

JSA released its Regional Roadmap, a framework assessing the jobs and skills system outside major cities. Regional employers report strong demand for skilled workers alongside persistent barriers to participation including housing, transport and care. For RTOs with regional delivery, this provides evidence-based data for funding applications and strategic planning.

URL: https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/news/new-report-highlights-opportunities-workers-regional-employers-seek-fill-jobs

### 4. FSO AI for VET compliance: Session 2 now open
*Source: ITECA News | 12 November 2026 | MEDIUM*

The Future Skills Organisation's AI action learning project for VET admin and compliance staff has entered Session 2 (validation and prioritisation). This is a structured, free program for trainers, assessors and compliance staff to engage with AI use cases in RTO settings. Session 1 launched in October. For RTO managers looking to reduce admin burden without large IT investment, this is a low-risk entry point.

URL: https://www.iteca.edu.au/web/news/Articles/2026/FSO%20Skills%20Accelerator-AI_AI%20for%20VET%20admin%20and%20compliance%20%E2%80%93%20Validation%20and%20prioritisation%20workshop.aspx

### 5. ITECA on regulatory incoherence: "Just Pick a Lane"
*Source: ITECA News | 9 November 2026 | MEDIUM*

ITECA's weekly commentary raised pointed concerns about the lack of agreement across government policy and regulatory agencies on what tertiary education regulation is for. The piece argues the dysfunction is costly and that students ultimately bear the higher bill. For RTOs navigating overlapping requirements from ASQA, state training authorities and DEWR, this reflects a persistent operational frustration — and signals continued sector pressure for rationalisation.

URL: https://www.iteca.edu.au/web/news/Articles/2026/National_Update_-_Tertiary%20Education%20Regulation%20Just%20Pick%20a%20Lane.aspx
