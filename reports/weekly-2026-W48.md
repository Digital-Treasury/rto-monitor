## Lead items

### 1. Universities Accord (Strengthening Governance) Bill 2026 enters Parliament
*TEQSA | 26 Nov 2026 | MEDIUM*

Minister Clare introduced the Universities Accord (Strengthening Governance) Bill 2026 into Parliament. It is directly relevant to dual-sector RTOs (registered with both ASQA and TEQSA) and signals the government's intent to legislate governance standards for tertiary providers. TEQSA will publish further updates as the bill progresses. Pure RTOs should watch the precedent: legislative governance mandates in higher ed often precede similar expectations in VET regulation.

URL: https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/universities-accord-strengthening-governance-bill-2026

### 4. ASIC director enforcement: appeal refused, new charge laid
*ASIC Newsroom | 23 Nov 2026 | MEDIUM*

Two items reinforcing director liability for RTO operators who are company directors:
- Full Federal Court refused former Berndale director Stavro D'Amore's appeal against his jail sentence for dishonesty offences. Criminal accountability stands on appeal.
- Former NSW director Philip Iacovou charged under s184 Corporations Act for dishonestly using his position to gain personal advantage.

Both cases apply equally to RTO directors: criminal obligations under the Corporations Act are unavoidable and do not disappear on appeal.

URLs:
- https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-291mr-berndale-director-stavro-d-amore-refused-leave-to-appeal-jail-sentence
